## 2026-09-30 — Phase 2 scoping kickoff

**Decision:** Phase 2 begins as a scoping/feasibility assessment, not a new regression.
No health-outcome model will be run until a single primary research direction is chosen.

**Why:** Phase 1's own conclusion was that exposure characterization — not sample size or
model sophistication — is the binding constraint. Jumping straight to a new outcome and a
new regression would repeat the mistake Phase 1 already diagnosed.

**What was done:** Inventoried 17 candidate exposure datasets and 12 candidate health
outcomes against explicit criteria (access, geography, temporal alignment, measured vs.
source-pressure, linkage quality). Scored three candidate research directions (A: focused
exposure–health study, B: exposure geography/source attribution, C: exposure-assignment
methodology) against a common set of criteria.

**Provisional recommendation:** Direction C (exposure-assignment sensitivity) as primary,
Direction B (source attribution, scoped to TRI/CWNS/Superfund) as secondary/parallel,
Direction A limited to the three free CDC PLACES additions (cholesterol, CHD, stroke) as
supporting evidence. Birth outcomes explicitly deferred — Texas DSHS data exist only at
county resolution, one aggregation level coarser than the ZCTA design.

**Status:** Provisional. See open items below and in memo §4. Not yet reviewed by the
research team.
